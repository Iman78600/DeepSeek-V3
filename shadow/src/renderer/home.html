Shadow

A browser that watches the web the way a security analyst would.

The analyst

Every page is scored before it loads and again after it renders. Phishing, fake support pages, cryptominers and pages that tell you to paste a command get stopped.

The firewall

Every request a page makes is filtered. Known-bad hosts, trackers, odd ports, and any attempt to reach your own network are dropped.

The sandbox

Downloads land in quarantine, stripped of the ability to run, and are taken apart before you are allowed to touch them.

Tor

Turn it on in settings to route everything through the Tor network. If Tor is not actually running, Shadow refuses to connect rather than quietly leak.

Every decision is made on this machine. Shadow does not send the pages you visit to any service. Press Ctrl+Shift+S for the SOC dashboard.
